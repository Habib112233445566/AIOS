**Completed:** 2026-08-21

## Benchmark table (real audit_client + retention modules, temp SQLite WAL DBs)

| Ring size | keep | verify (live) | rotate | verify post-rotate (live=1001) | verify --full (archive replay) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10,000 rows | 1,000 | **0.574 s** | 1.327 s | 0.047 s | 0.344 s (9,000 archived) |
| 100,000 rows | 1,000 | **6.251 s** | 25.898 s | 0.151 s | 6.021 s (99,000 archived) |

Derived per-row costs (linear region):
- `verify` live walk: ~60 µs/row (canonical-JSON re-hash, Python)
- `verify --full` archive replay: ~60 µs/row (same work over JSONL)
- `rotate`: ~150–260 µs/archived row (includes pre-rotate verify, JSONL
  serialization, bloom build, file write, SQL delete+insert)

Extra finding: `VACUUM` after rotation does not change verify time
(0.055 s vs 0.062 s for 1001 rows) — post-rotation live verify cost is
bounded purely by `keep_rows`, not by prior table size.

## Recommended retention cadence

- **Rotate threshold: 10,000 live rows.** At that size `verify` stays
  under ~0.6 s (interactive) and `rotate` completes in ~1.3 s.
- **Keep window: 1,000 rows.** Post-rotation verify then costs ~0.05 s.
- Above ~50k un-rotated rows, rotation itself exceeds 10 s and verify
  exceeds 3 s — do not let the ring grow past the threshold.
- `verify --full` is an audit/DR operation (replays all archives);
  schedule it periodically or on incident, not on the hot path.
- Rotation is operator-initiated only (CLI / MCP-with-grant); no
  automatic background rotation in Sprint 3.
